Warranty Structure

Lifetime Structural Guarantee

Wall anchoring integrity, rod/track failure, and structural joinery — for the duration of homeownership.

5-Year Mechanical Warranty

Soft-close drawer slides, hinges, fold-down mechanisms, edge-banding.

Weight Limits

200 lbs/linear foot — Tier 1
100 lbs/linear foot — Tiers 2 & 3

Given routine humidity near the washer/dryer, contact points (bracket clips, rod mounts) spec galvanized steel rather than powder coat/epoxy alone — coating wear at a moving joint is a real rust risk here, not just cosmetic. Contact points use nylon/Delrin bushings to slow wear, and field techs carry a touch-up pen for warranty-visit spot repair.
